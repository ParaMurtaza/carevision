Assisted Intelligence

Two ways AI helps you

Our assistive technology has two manifestations — one for the doctor, one for the patient — working in unison.

🩺

For the Doctor

CareVision AI helps patients provide the right information predictively. It equips doctors with a structured, pre-analyzed case brief — so they arrive at the consultation already informed, ready to diagnose faster and with greater precision.

🧬

For the Patient

The AI guides you through your symptoms intelligently, asking only the most relevant questions. It aids doctors to investigate each case so the diagnosis is quicker and sharper — your doctor knows your condition before they've even seen you.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the AI a doctor? Can I rely on its diagnosis?

No. CareVision AI gives pre-screening information to help you and your doctor save time. It never gives a final diagnosis. A qualified doctor must confirm every finding, and in an emergency you should call 112.

How does the "best doctor" recommendation work?

Doctors are scored on how well their speciality matches your AI result, patient rating, completed consultations, free appointment slots (more weight when your case is urgent) and fee. The report shows the reasons for each suggestion.

What files can I upload for analysis?

PDF, JPG, PNG or WEBP up to 5 MB — lab reports, X-ray, CT or MRI images (exported as pictures) and skin photos. Use clear, well-lit photos for the best result.

Who can see my reports and health data?

Only you, and the doctor you booked with. Doctors cannot see patients who did not book with them. The admin panel shows business statistics and orders, not your medical reports.

How do I get a doctor or admin account?

Patients can sign up for free. Doctor accounts are created by the clinic admin for security, so doctors receive their login from the clinic.

How do video consultations work?

After you book, you get a private video-room link. Open it from your dashboard at your appointment time — no app to install.

Can I cancel an appointment or an order?

Yes. Open your dashboard and press Cancel on any appointment or pending order.

Are the prices real?

Prices shown for tests, scans, plans and medicines are demo values for this project.
